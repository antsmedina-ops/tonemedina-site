Biography

Denver-based artist with 20 years of experience as a professional artist. Holding a BFA Communication Design from MSU, rooted in tradition, focused on the ephemeral.

His art merges the digital realm with tradational interpretations of the ephemeral, extending perceptions.

Recently designs focusing on the ephermeral nature of life, art & the intersectionality they share, Intersecting identities do not simply add to one another they compound and interact to create entirely new, distinct experiences.

In addition to studio work, he's been a performance artist, muralist participating locally & regionally.
