Biography

Tone Medina is a Denver-based artist. with over 20 years of experience as a professional artist. With a background in communication design, my art tends to stay rooted in the traditional with a focus on the ephemeral world of street painting. I've since expanded to include digital creations as well as the immersive new media. By blending physical artistry with modern technology. Currently exploring how digital tools can enhance the visual narrative of community-focused art. I'm Interested pushing the boundaries of perception.
