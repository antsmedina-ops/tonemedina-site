Biography

Tone Medina is a Denver-based artist with a B.F.A. Communication Design fromMetropolitan State University with over 20 years of experience as a professional chalk artist. While deeply rooted in the traditional and ephemeral world of street painting, he has expanded his practice to include digital creation and immersive new media, such as VR and AR. By blending two decades of physical artistry with modern technology, He explores how digital tools can enhance the visual narrative of community-focused art. He is currently dedicated to pushing the boundaries of perception, using mixed reality to bridge the gap between traditional techniques and digital art.
